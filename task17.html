Company Profile

Moonton Games

Moonton Games is a leading mobile game developer established in 2014 and headquartered in Shanghai, China. The company is best known for creating Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, one of the world's most popular multiplayer online battle arena games. Its mission is to deliver exciting gaming experiences while connecting players across the globe. Moonton also promotes competitive esports through international tournaments and continuously develops innovative mobile entertainment for millions of players.

About the Company

Founded in 2014, Moonton Games has grown into one of the world's leading mobile game companies. The company operates in several countries and focuses on delivering high-quality multiplayer games. Through continuous innovation and community engagement, Moonton has built a strong global gaming ecosystem.

Products & Services

  • Mobile Legends: Bang Bang – Multiplayer Online Battle Arena (MOBA) game.
  • Magic Chess: Go Go – Strategy-based auto chess mobile game.
  • Esports Management – Organizes MPL, M-Series and international tournaments.
  • Silver and Blood – Fantasy role-playing mobile game.

Achievements

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang has surpassed 1.5 billion downloads worldwide and attracts millions of active players every month. The game has become one of the biggest mobile esports titles, featuring prestigious tournaments such as the M-Series World Championship and MPL leagues across multiple countries.
